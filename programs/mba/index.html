Frequently asked

Is the MBA degree UGC recognised?

Yes. All degree programs are approved by the state higher education council and the curriculum follows the NEP 2020 credit framework.

Can I change my campus after admission?

Campus transfer is allowed once, at the start of the second year, subject to seat availability and the dean's approval.

How are scholarships applied?

Merit and need-based scholarships are auto-evaluated from your application. Swami Vivekananda and OBC/SC/ST waivers need the uploaded certificate.

Do you help with hostel allotment?

Yes, hostel preference is collected during enrolment and allotment is confirmed within 7 working days of fee payment.
